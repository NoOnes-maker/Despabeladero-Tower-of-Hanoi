% Base case: Move 1 disk directly

move(1, X, Y, _) :-

write('Move top disk from '), write(X),

write(' to '), write(Y), nl.

% Recursive case: Move N disks

move(N, X, Y, Z) :-

N > 1,

M is N - 1,

move(M, X, Z, Y),

move(1, X, Y, _),

move(M, Z, Y, X).

Prolog Execution Logic:

  • X = Source Peg (left)
  • Y = Target Peg (right)
  • Z = Auxiliary Peg (center)

Prolog recursively evaluates how to move N disks onto the target peg. During execution, each single move statement lifts the top disk above the stick height, slides it horizontally, and drops it on top of the destination stick.
